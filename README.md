# FBA Ledger Audit

Find lost or warehouse-damaged Amazon FBA units that still need investigation after found inventory and reimbursements are accounted for. The free auditor reads your inventory reports in your browser. It requires no Seller Central credentials.

**[Run the free FBA Inventory Ledger audit](https://fba-ledger-audit.pages.dev/)** or choose **Try sample data** on the homepage to see a worked case before selecting any files.

## How do I find lost FBA inventory that hasn’t been reimbursed?

Use an Inventory Ledger Summary or Detailed CSV for one store and marketplace. Add a Reimbursements CSV covering the same period. The auditor matches identifiers, deducts offsets and ranks remaining events by filing deadline. Without the Reimbursements CSV, you still need to check prior payments yourself.

For example, 9 lost units plus 4 damaged, less 1 found and 3 reimbursed, leaves **9 units to investigate**. At an illustrative manufacturing cost of $15 per unit, that gives a **$135 estimate**. Replace the default with your own sourced manufacturing cost. Amazon determines eligibility and the actual payment.
