Calculation method

How to read the result

Multiply the three packaged dimensions in inches and divide by your divisor. Compare the result in pounds with the measured scale weight. Recheck the package against the measurement record before pricing a discrepancy.

Worked example

A 14 × 10 × 4 inch package occupies 560 cubic inches. At an entered divisor of 139, its dimensional weight is 4.03 lb.

Seller questions

Common questions

Is 139 the right divisor for every shipment?

139 is the starting calculation requested for this worksheet. Verify the applicable divisor, tier and rounding in your own fee schedule.

Can I calculate an overcharge from dimensions alone?

You also need the actual billed fee and the applicable fee for your verified measurements. This tool supplies the weight comparison for that review.
