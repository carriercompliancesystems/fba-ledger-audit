Calculation method

How to read the result

Count the refund date as day zero. Under the general guidance, wait through day 45, review from day 46 through day 120, and confirm the live eligibility in your account. Check the physical return and reimbursement records.

Worked example

For a November 1, 2026 refund, day 46 is December 17, 2026 and day 120 is March 1, 2027. A review on December 16 is still within the waiting period.

Seller questions

Common questions

What if the customer returned the item?

Match the return and any reimbursement to the original refund. A date falling inside the window alone does not establish that units are missing.

Which Seller Central path should I use?

Open support from the relevant order or refund and confirm the exact current menu label, claim category and eligibility before submitting. Navigation and case handling can differ.
