Seller questions

Common questions

Why can the balance differ from the lost-unit calculation?

A balance uses all relevant movements. Lost and damaged reconciliation uses those event counts and offsets. An omitted movement can change the balance without establishing a loss.

How should I enter customer shipments?

Use the signed movement. For 180 units shipped out enter −180. Enter lost and damaged fields as positive counts because the formula subtracts them.
