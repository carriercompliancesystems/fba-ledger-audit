Calculation method

How to read the result

Multiply stranded units by unit cost for tied-up inventory value. Separately multiply units, modeled days and your daily holding cost to estimate carrying expense. Adding the two would mix an asset value with an expense.

Worked example

Thirty-five units at $15 tie up $525. At an illustrative $0.05 per unit per day, holding them for 20 days costs $35.

Seller questions

Common questions

Does stranded inventory count as lost inventory?

Stranded status alone gives no evidence of a physical loss. Check the listing and inventory records before treating units as missing.

Can I compare resolution timelines?

Change the modeled days to compare the added carrying cost of a slower resolution. Keep unit cost fixed when comparing the same inventory.
