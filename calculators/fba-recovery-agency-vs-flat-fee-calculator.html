Calculation method

How to read the result

Multiply the recovered amount by the entered agency rate and compare it with the $29 one-time price. The difference can be positive or negative. This comparison assumes the same recovery outcome.

Worked example

At $400 actually recovered, an illustrative 25% fee is $100. The $29 flat fee leaves $71 more. At $80 recovered, the flat fee costs $9 more.

Seller questions

Common questions

At what amount are the fees equal?

At 25% the break-even is $116 actually recovered. For another positive percentage, divide $29 by the rate expressed as a decimal.

Is 25% a quote for an agency?

The percentage is illustrative; agency rates vary. Enter the rate in an actual offer and compare the services and terms as well as the fee.
