Calculation method

How to read the result

Add lost and warehouse-damaged units, deduct found and reimbursed units, and floor the open quantity at zero. Multiply by sourced manufacturing cost. Keep all four counts on the same SKU and period.

Policy background supplied for this tool. Reimbursement valuation and windows

Worked example

Nine lost plus four damaged units, less one found and three reimbursed, leaves nine open units. At an illustrative $15 per unit, the estimate is $135.
