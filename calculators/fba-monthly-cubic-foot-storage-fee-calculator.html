Seller questions

Common questions

How do I convert inches to cubic feet?

Multiply length, width and height in inches, then divide by 1,728. One cubic foot contains 1,728 cubic inches.

Which aged surcharge should I enter?

Use the applicable incremental per-cubic-foot rate on your own statement. Leave the aged inputs at zero for base storage alone. A per-unit minimum or another billing basis needs a separate calculation.
