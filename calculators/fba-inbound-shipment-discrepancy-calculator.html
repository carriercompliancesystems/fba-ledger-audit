Calculation method

How to read the result

Subtract checked-in units from delivered units. Price a positive shortfall at your own assumed unit value. The date checker adds nine calendar months to delivery and uses the last day of the target month if needed.

Worked example

A delivery of 240 units with 222 checked in leaves an 18-unit gap. At a seller-entered $10, the inventory exposure is $180. Eligibility still depends on the shipment record.

Seller questions

Common questions

Is the nine-month date confirmed for my shipment?

Nine months from delivery is general guidance. Verify current eligibility, the exact menu label and the date shown for your shipment in Seller Central before submitting.

How does this differ from an FC ledger loss?

An inbound shortage compares delivery and receiving counts. A fulfillment-center ledger adjustment uses its own event date and the separate 60-day checker.
