Calculation method

How to read the result

Calculate each percentage on the same fee basis, then multiply the difference by the number of equivalent transactions. A positive result points to a possible overcharge. A negative result means the entered billed rate was lower.

Worked example

On twenty $50 fee bases, illustrative rates of 15% and 12% yield $150 and $120. The $30 difference is the amount to trace to transaction records.

Seller questions

Common questions

Can I use this for a category with minimum or tiered fees?

Use rates that accurately describe the transactions you enter. Split different tiers or use separate invoice calculations when a minimum changes the fee.

What evidence supports a category review?

Keep the charged transaction, the item category and the fee schedule applicable on the transaction date. Check the current Seller Central support label before filing.
