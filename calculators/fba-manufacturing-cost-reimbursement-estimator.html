Calculation method

How to read the result

Divide the sourced manufacturing amount by the matching units manufactured. Multiply the unrounded unit estimate by the unresolved quantity. Check the admissible cost components and documentation for your account.

Worked example

A $2,400 manufacturing amount across 200 units gives $12 per unit. Nine unresolved units at that cost have an estimated value of $108.

Seller questions

Common questions

Why does this worksheet use batch cost?

A matching batch amount and unit count give you a documented basis for the estimate. Retail price or a margin target would answer a different question.

What if the batch contains multiple SKUs?

Separate the documented manufacturing amount and quantity by SKU before dividing. A blended figure can misstate the cost of the missing item.
