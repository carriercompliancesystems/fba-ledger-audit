Calculation method

How to read the result

Estimated supply equals available units divided by average daily shipments. The top-up estimate rounds the unit shortfall up. Fee exposure uses the assessed quantity and rate you supply. A days-of-supply estimate alone cannot establish fee eligibility.

Worked example

With 200 available units and 10 shipped daily, estimated supply is 20 days. Reaching a 28-day target at the same pace takes 80 more units.

Seller questions

Common questions

Does being below 28 days prove I owe a fee?

This worksheet uses 28 days as an editable benchmark. Check the historical metrics, eligibility and exemptions shown for your SKU in Seller Central.

What if daily shipments are zero?

Days of supply has no finite value at a zero shipment rate. Choose a period with a meaningful nonzero average or review the item as inactive.
