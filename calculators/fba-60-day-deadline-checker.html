Calculation method

How to read the result

Add 60 calendar days to the ledger event date. The event date is day zero. This tool includes day 60 as due today and marks an event expired from day 61. Confirm the event date against your source report.

Policy background supplied for this tool. Reimbursement valuation and windows · Filing window change

Worked example

An event dated November 10, 2026 reaches day 60 on January 9, 2027. On December 27 it has 13 calendar days left.
