Audit guide

Common questions

Which reports do I need?

Use an Inventory Ledger Summary or Detailed CSV for one store. Add the matching Reimbursements CSV to deduct units already paid. Use the same marketplace and reporting period.

How are recovery estimates calculated?

The estimate multiplies unresolved units by their sourced manufacturing cost. The editable $15.00 starting cost is illustrative. Replace it with your own costs before relying on a dollar estimate. Amazon determines reimbursement.

What does the 60-day window cover?

For fulfillment-center lost or warehouse-damaged inventory, count 60 calendar days from the ledger event or adjustment. Confirm the underlying event date if your Summary report groups a period.

What do I get for $29?

A one-time payment unlocks a ranked audit CSV and editable Seller Central case scripts in this browser profile. You review the evidence and submit cases yourself.

Who can see my CSVs?

Your browser reads the files locally. The auditor sends no CSV, SKU, cost or finding to a server. Stripe handles checkout. This site sends only the returned checkout session ID to its payment verification endpoint.
